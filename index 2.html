Build your syllabus

Subjects → Chapters → 3 tasks
Each chapter has three required tasks: watched lecture, read notes and read book. Completing all three marks the chapter complete and automatically schedules its revision. Then test yourself with practice questions and log the score: chapters under 70% show up as weak on your exam cards.

Revision schedule

Auto-created when a chapter is completed
Each completed chapter gets revision dates at +1, +3, +7 and +21 days. You have a 2-day grace period after each due date. Revise by recall: close your notes, write or say what you remember, then check.
